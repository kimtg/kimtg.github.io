Key Features

Official ClojureScript Engine: Uses official browser ClojureScript evaluation with persistent namespaces, atoms, vectors, hash-maps, macros, and full standard library.

Rainbow Parentheses: All matched delimiters `()`, `[]`, `{}` cycle distinct high-contrast colors corresponding to their AST nesting depth.

Parentheses Matching Highlight: Highlights paired brackets instantly as the cursor moves, with mismatched bracket error highlighting.

Jump to Definition: Hover over any custom function or variable while holding `Ctrl` (or `Cmd`) to see its signature; click to navigate instantly to its definition line!
